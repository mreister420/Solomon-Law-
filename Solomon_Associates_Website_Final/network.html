Commercial Model

A simple network model designed for growth

Solomon & Associates Inc owns the brand, client intake process, marketing platform, CRM, client service standards, matter allocation and relationship management. Independent lawyers keep their own practices but participate through a network agreement.

The network is designed to provide referrals, templates, client onboarding support, brand presence, admin support, matter tracking, marketing, panel access and institutional tender positioning.

Professional note

The final commercial model will be structured in line with applicable South African legal practice rules and professional requirements. Fee arrangements, platform fees, referral structures, administration fees and centralised service margins must be reviewed for compliance before implementation.

Strategic Advantage

National reach without losing local independence

The Solomon Property Law Network allows Solomon & Associates Inc to build regional presence without opening expensive physical offices in every province. It creates the ability to demonstrate broader coverage for property panels, developer mandates, enterprise development programmes, bank work, union housing work and property ecosystem opportunities.

For independent lawyers, the network creates an opportunity to access pipeline, administration support, brand credibility and coordinated systems without becoming employees of a corporate law firm.
