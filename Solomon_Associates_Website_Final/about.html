About Solomon & Associates Inc.

Solomon & Associates Inc. is built on a simple but powerful belief: legal services should be professional, strategic and deeply personal. The firm provides focused support across property law, conveyancing, litigation, arbitration, commercial law and estate administration.

Our Mission

Professional service delivered with empathy and precision.

Solomon & Associates Inc. is a boutique law firm established in 2024 by Attorney and Conveyancer Shanay Solomon. The firm was founded with a vision to deliver legal services that are both highly professional and deeply personal.

Our focus areas include Conveyancing, Estate Administration, Commercial Law, Litigation and Arbitration. We pride ourselves on crafting bespoke legal strategies with empathy, clarity and precision. Clients benefit from direct access to an experienced practitioner who understands both the law and the human story behind each matter.

Our Team

Meet the team

Founder & Director | Attorney & Conveyancer

Shanay Solomon

Shanay Solomon is the Founder and Director of Solomon & Associates Inc, a boutique law firm based in Bloemfontein, South Africa. With a strong commitment to legal excellence and client-centred service, she has established a practice that combines professional expertise with a personalised and practical approach to legal matters.

Shanay holds a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) degree from the University of the Free State. She was admitted as an Attorney in 2023 and as a Conveyancer in 2024, the same year she founded Solomon & Associates Inc. Her areas of practice include property law and conveyancing, commercial law, litigation, arbitration and estate administration.

Recognising that every legal matter comes with its own circumstances and challenges, Shanay places great importance on understanding her clients' needs, communicating clearly and providing carefully considered legal guidance. Her approach is grounded in integrity, attention to detail and a genuine commitment to building lasting professional relationships.

As Founder and Director, Shanay continues to lead the firm with a forward-thinking vision, focused on maintaining high professional standards, embracing innovation and expanding access to quality legal services while preserving the personal attention that defines Solomon & Associates Inc.

Executive – Litigation

Sinethemba Betela

Sinethemba is an Executive in the Litigation Department at Solomon & Associates Inc., bringing extensive experience in legal research, litigation and legal strategy.

He obtained his LLB degree from the University of the Western Cape in 2018 and began his career as a Law Researcher at the Supreme Court of Appeal, where he supported Judges through in-depth legal research and the verification of legal authorities. He subsequently completed his articles of clerkship at Symington De Kok Attorneys, gaining experience in Magistrates’ Court and High Court litigation.

Admitted as an attorney in 2021, Sinethemba joined Munnik Basson Dagama Attorneys, where he was promoted to Head of Department in 2022. His professional experience reflects a strong foundation in legal analysis, litigation management and the development of effective legal strategies.

At Solomon & Associates Inc., he brings a considered, analytical and solutions-driven approach to litigation, with a focus on protecting clients’ interests and delivering effective legal outcomes.
