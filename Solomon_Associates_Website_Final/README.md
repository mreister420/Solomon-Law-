# Solomon & Associates Inc Website

Static responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript. Open index.html locally or upload the production files to public_html. No Node.js, npm dependencies, framework, environment variables, build process or Vercel service is required.

## Structure
- index.html: Home
- about.html: About and Team (Shanay Solomon and Sinethemba Betela)
- services.html: Conveyancing, Estate Administration, Commercial Law and Litigation
- network.html: Solomon Property Law Network and application form
- news.html: Media hub and forthcoming articles
- faq.html: General questions
- contact.html: Consultation enquiry and contact details
- styles.css / script.js: Shared styling and interactive behaviour
- assets/: Optimised WebP photos, mobile variants and logos
- robots.txt / sitemap.xml: Domain-specific SEO
- DEPLOYMENT.md: Upload guide, limitations and post-launch checklist
- QA-REPORT.md: Local verification and remaining launch checks

## Handover
Use Solomon_Associates_Website_Final.zip or the folder of the same name. Upload its contents, not the outer folder, to public_html. No dist folder or npm command is necessary. See DEPLOYMENT.md.

## Enquiries
Contact and chatbot prepare WhatsApp summaries; the visitor must press Send. Network applications prepare an email draft and require an email client. Consent is required. No backend automatically delivers or stores enquiries. The chatbot is scripted, not an AI legal advisor.
